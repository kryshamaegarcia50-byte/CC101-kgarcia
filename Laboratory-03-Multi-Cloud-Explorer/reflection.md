# Mission Reflection

After exploring AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud Platform (GCP). I found its focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes interesting, especially because these technologies are becoming more important in the IT industry. I also liked how GCP provides different services that can support both small projects and larger applications.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide important cloud services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, security, and identity management. They also allow businesses to scale their resources depending on their needs. Even though their services have different names and features, they all help organizations reduce their dependence on physical infrastructure and make their applications more accessible and flexible.

Business requirements have a big influence on cloud platform selection. A company needs to consider its budget, existing technologies, security needs, performance, scalability, and future plans before choosing a provider. For example, Azure may be a better choice for a company that already uses Microsoft products, while GCP may be more suitable for an organization focused on AI and Machine Learning.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud platform is not simply about picking the most popular provider. I learned that I need to understand the client's actual needs first and then match those needs with the right cloud services.

After completing this mission, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has become more organized and meaningful. I added research, comparison tables, recommendations, a decision matrix, Linux investigation, and my reflection. Overall, this activity helped me understand cloud computing better while also improving my confidence in using GitHub and presenting technical information.
